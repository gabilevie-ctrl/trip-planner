# New-trip wizard

Each step is one screen; the left rail shows progress; every step can be revisited. Nothing is required to see results: skipping a step uses sensible defaults and says so.

1. **Basics** — trip name · number of travellers (names optional) · origin city · dates (exact, or a window plus a length) · budget (total or per head, currency) · lodging style (B&B/Airbnb vs hotels).
2. **Who's coming and their limits** — per traveller: altitude ceiling, heat ceiling, crowd tolerance, mobility, no-flights preference, "don't put me on a full-hike trip" (max share of hiking days). Plus a shared list of places already visited.
3. **What kind of trip** — sliders for city / mountains / beach / wildlife / history / food / festival; pace (slow ↔ packed); must-haves in words.
4. **Destinations** — the library scored against steps 1–3, with search, filters, compare table, weighted ranking, and "ideas to explore" for places not in the library. Each card explains its score in a sentence. Pick one (or shortlist two to build out).
5. **Build** — blocks and days per destination (cities / mountains / beach / rest), presets, the budget table, warnings (over length, over budget, limits broken, mostly hiking).
6. **Book** — generated from the itinerary:
   - Travel: prefilled links (Google Flights, Rome2Rio, Trainline/Rail Europe) for the outbound, return and internal legs.
   - Lodging: one line per stop with dates, guests and rooms, and Booking/Airbnb search links; the block's "where to stay" tip.
   - Permits, huts, guides, boats: checklist with lead times; tick as done.
   - Nothing is booked by the app.
7. **Share** — copy link (state in URL), export JSON, print view for the people who just want to read it.

Home screen: list of trips in this browser, "New trip", "Import from link or file".
