Ideas to explore

Add a destination the family wants looked at. It appears in the comparison as a "to explore" row with whatever rough cost you give it, until it is built out properly.

Budgets are per person in euros, flights or trains from Brussels, built from typical 2026 prices and the way you travel: a local B&B or an Airbnb for the five of you in two rooms (so lodging is €25–60 per person per night depending on the country), eating out once a day and self-catering the rest, a shared hire car or a van with driver where that is the sensible way to move five people, huts, yurts, cabins or guesthouses on the trail, a local guide only for the multi-day hikes that need one, museums and local transport, plus 8% contingency. "Hotels" in the top bar switches to a three-star hotel room per couple and restaurants every meal, about 40% more. Treat them as ±25% planning figures, not quotes. Days not assigned to a block count as travel and rest days. Temperatures are typical afternoon highs for the dates; "nights" is the coldest you will sleep. Altitude is the highest point reached in a block. Transport hours are the typical time to reach a block from the previous hub, by car, train, boat or plane including airport time. "Guidebook pick" marks places that both Lonely Planet and the Routard treat as the destination's highlights; the links go to their country pages for the detail.
